# Roots & Remedy Agri Guide 🌱

A free, open-source, picture-based crop guide for farmers in Lesotho.

Pick your **district**, pick your **crop**, and get:

- 🐛 **Pest & disease risk** for the month, ranked High / Watch / Low for your district's climate
- 🛡️ **Integrated Pest Management (IPM)** steps: prevent → check → natural control → chemicals last
- 🌱 **Fertiliser programme** by growth stage
- 🧴 **Spray days**: safety tips and a 7-day good/bad spray forecast (live weather from Open-Meteo)
- 🇱🇸 English / Sesotho menus

Crops: tomato, green pepper, cabbage, maize, potatoes.
Products are linked to stock at **Foso Hardware (PTY) LTD**. Every recommendation ends with
*"Confirm at the Foso Hardware front desk."*

## Credits
- District boundaries: [geoBoundaries](https://www.geoboundaries.org) (CC BY 4.0)
- Weather: [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com)

## Disclaimer
Educational guide only. Always read and follow the product label (rate, safety gear, days before harvest).
Recommendations should be reviewed by a qualified agronomist before use.

## Run it
It's a single `index.html` — open it in a browser, or deploy the folder to Netlify.
